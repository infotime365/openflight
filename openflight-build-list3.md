# OpenFlight build list by layer

A simple, layer-by-layer shopping list for the OpenFlight launch monitor. Battery powered, with a detachable wall-adapter jack and the TI angle radar.

## Layer 1: Case with power

**Case: print**

- Shell
- Screen frame
- Radar front
- Mic front
- Mic holder
- Feet

**Case: buy**

- Brass inserts M3: https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/SI/IUTB-M3
- Brass inserts M2.5: https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/SI/IUTB-M2.5
- Brass inserts M2: https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/SI/IUTB-M2
- Screws M3x12: https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/Keystone-Electronics/29314
- Screws M2.5x6: https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/Keystone-Electronics/29301

**Power: buy**

- Battery board (Geekworm X1202): https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CRZ4ZXQW
- 4 batteries (flat-top 18650, unprotected) 🔴 LINK NEEDED
- 12 volt adapter 🔴 LINK NEEDED
- Panel jack: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DP6MNQQB
- Heat shrink or heat shrink solder sleeves (solder the panel jack wires straight to the board) 🔴 LINK NEEDED
- Power button 🔴 LINK NEEDED

## Layer 2: The computer

- Raspberry Pi 5: https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/Raspberry-Pi/SC1111
- Cooling fan: https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/active-cooler/
- microSD card (already have one)

## Layer 3: The screen

- 7 inch 1024x600 screen with a ribbon cable (DSI) connection (still to be chosen) 🔴 LINK NEEDED
- Ribbon cable for the screen: https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/Raspberry-Pi/SC1131

## Layer 4: The radar

- OPS243-A radar (USB version): https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/OmniPreSense/OPS243-A-CW-RP

## Layer 5: TI angle radar

- TI IWR6843LEVM radar board: https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/Texas-Instruments/IWR6843LEVM
- USB cable that carries data: https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/Raspberry-Pi/SC0732
- 4 jumper wires: https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/Adafruit/1953
- LIS3DH tilt sensor: https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/Adafruit/2809
- STEMMA QT to female sockets cable: https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/SparkFun-Electronics/CAB-17261

## Layer 6: The mic

- SparkFun sound detector (SEN-14262): https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/SparkFun-Electronics/SEN-14262
- 47k ohm resistor (kit): https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/SparkFun-Electronics/COM-10969
- 3 jumper wires: https://www.mouser.com/en/ProductDetail/SparkFun-Electronics/PRT-12796 (solder the GATE wire and cover it with heat shrink)
