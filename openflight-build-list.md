# OpenFlight build list by layer

A simple, layer-by-layer shopping list for the OpenFlight launch monitor. Battery powered, with a detachable wall-adapter jack and the TI angle radar.

## Layer 1: Case with power

**Case: print**

- Shell
- Screen frame
- Radar front
- Mic front
- Mic holder
- Feet

**Case: buy**

- Small brass inserts and screws (sizes to be settled)

**Power: buy**

- Battery board (Geekworm X1202): https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CRZ4ZXQW
- 4 batteries (flat-top 18650, unprotected)
- 12 volt adapter
- Panel jack: https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DP6MNQQB
- Heat shrink or heat shrink solder sleeves (solder the panel jack wires straight to the board)
- Power button

## Layer 2: The computer

- Raspberry Pi 5: https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/raspberry-pi-5/
- Cooling fan: https://www.raspberrypi.com/products/active-cooler/
- microSD card

## Layer 3: The screen

- 7 inch 1024x600 screen with a ribbon cable (DSI) connection (still to be chosen)
- Ribbon cable for the screen

## Layer 4: The radar

- OPS243-A radar (USB version): https://www.mouser.com/new/omnipresense/omnipresense-ops243-a-radar-sensor/

## Layer 5: TI angle radar

- TI IWR6843LEVM radar board: https://www.mouser.com/new/texas-instruments/ti-iwr6843levm-evaluation-module/
- USB cable that carries data
- 4 jumper wires
- LIS3DH tilt sensor
- STEMMA QT to female sockets cable

## Layer 6: The mic

- SparkFun sound detector (SEN-14262): https://www.sparkfun.com/products/14262
- 47k ohm resistor
- 3 jumper wires (solder the GATE wire and cover it with heat shrink)
